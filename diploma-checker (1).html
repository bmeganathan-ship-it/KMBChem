Subjects

The Core

Theory of Knowledge

About 100 hours on how we know what we claim to know. Assessed by an exhibition (one third: three objects, up to 950 words) and an essay on a prescribed title (two thirds: up to 1,600 words).

Extended Essay

Independent research of up to 4,000 words in one subject, about 40 hours of work, with three reflection sessions with a supervisor (the last is a short viva voce). A new guide applies from May 2027.

Creativity, Activity, Service

About 18 months of experiences across creativity, activity and service. Not graded, but every requirement must be met.
