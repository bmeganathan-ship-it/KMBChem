Build my plan

Choose one subject in each block

Subjects in the same block are taught at the same time, so you can take only one of them. The blocks follow the November 2026 Grade 11 and 12 exam schedule.

HL = Higher Level: more depth and more hours. SL = Standard Level. Choose 3 subjects at HL and 3 at SL.

About Visual Arts: you can choose it in Block 4 or Block 5, in place of one science, but only once. Visual Arts has no written exam, so it is not on the exam schedule; the DP Coordinator confirms its exact block each year.

      Other routes

      When the full Diploma is not the right fit

      IB courses + BMIS High School Diploma

      Students can take 5 or 6 IB courses without the full Diploma, alongside the BMIS High School Diploma (HSD). Some subjects have an HSD version, which removes the most demanding 30% of the course and adds a skills-based component.

      HSD versions: Business Management, Design Technology, Maths: Applications & Interpretation, Visual Arts, SEHS.

      Online subjects (Pamoja)

      A subject not taught at school can be studied online through Pamoja, an IB-approved provider. Families pay the course fee.

      • Economics, Psychology, Digital Society, Philosophy
      • Film
      • Maths: Applications & Interpretation HL
      • French ab initio, Mandarin ab initio

      As listed in the 2025–26 High School Pathways Handbook.

      Your home language

      Students with a strong home language other than English can study it as a school-supported self-taught Literature SL course in Group 1.

      Will every subject run?

      Some subjects may not run if very few students choose them. The school confirms the final list after students hand in their choices.
