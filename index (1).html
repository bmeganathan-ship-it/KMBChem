Core numbers

Cheiro reads three numbers together: the birth (psychic) number shows the inner nature, the destiny number shows the direction of life, and the name number shows how others meet the person.

Pythagorean numbers

The Western method used by most online calculators: A=1 to I=9, repeating. Y counts as a consonant. 11, 22 and 33 are kept as master numbers.

Namology

Cheiro's letter values (Chaldean). Every letter in the name is counted.

About this app

Numerology is a traditional reading, not a measured test.

  • Number sheet: taken from the numerology sheet, with spellings tidied. Saturn (8) is given Saturday, its usual day.
  • Year meanings: short summaries written for this app from the yearly-forecast method in Numerology: With Tantra, Ayurveda, and Astrology by Harish Johari. They are not copies of the book's text.
  • Colours: each main colour comes from the number sheet, with one traditional extra shade per number, such as orange next to gold.
  • Weekday numbers: the forecast numbers each weekday by its ruling planet (Tuesday = Mars = 9) unless you switch to the simple count.
  • Using it with students: treat the numbers as a way to start a conversation, alongside their interests and results, rather than as the reason for a subject or career choice.
  • Sharing: the app is private until it is shared from its Share menu. Saved people stay only in this browser.
  • Source: the Pythagorean numbers follow the method used by numerologycalc.org.
