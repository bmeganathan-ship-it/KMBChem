Worked example: logging a day
  1. Set a timer for 22 minutes and do 15 Paper 1A questions with no notes.
  2. Mark them with the answer key. Suppose you got 11 right.
  3. Enter Questions done: 15 and Correct: 11. The tracker shows 73%.
  4. Tick the topics you met, for example Reactivity 1 and Structure 2.
  5. Write one line about a mistake, then press Save this day.

For Paper 1B and Paper 2, enter the marks you scored and the marks available from the mark scheme. 3 questions worth 14 marks, scored 9 → 64%.

    Steady beats intense. Twenty-five focused minutes every day will do more than a long weekend cram, and it leaves room for your IA, EE, other subjects and university applications.
